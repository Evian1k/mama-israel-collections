# Mama Israel Collections — Owner's Simple Guide

Karibu! This guide explains everything YOU need to run your shop.
You do not need any technical knowledge. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use this.

---

## 1. How to open your shop's admin panel

Your admin panel is private — customers never see it.

1. Open your shop's website on your phone or computer.
2. **Tap the logo at the top-left corner 5 times quickly** (within 3 seconds).
3. A private sign-in page appears.
4. Type your admin email and password, then tap **Sign in**.

> Your sign-in email and starting password were given to you privately (not in
> this document). Keep them safe.

## 2. Change your password (do this the first time)

The **first time you sign in**, the shop will ask you to create your own new
password. This is normal — it keeps your shop safe.

- Type the starting password in **Current password**.
- Choose a new password that is **at least 10 characters** and has **letters and numbers**.
- Type it again to confirm, then tap **Save and continue**.

You can change it again any time: **Admin → Settings → Admin email or password**.
(You can also change your sign-in email there.)

**Never share your password with anyone.**

## 3. Add a product by hand

1. Sign in and tap **Products** → **New product**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Product name** (e.g. "Floral Wrap Dress")
   - **Description** — a few friendly sentences
   - **Category** — choose where it belongs (e.g. Dresses, Skirts)
   - **Price (KSh)**
   - **Sizes** — tap the size buttons (S, M, L, XL…)
   - **Colours** — optional, tap a colour to add it
   - **Stock quantity** — how many pieces you have
3. Upload photos (see next section).
4. Tap **Save product**.

Your product appears in the shop immediately. Customers can buy it right away.

## 4. Add a product with the AI Product Assistant ✨

This is the fastest way to add products — you just describe the item like you
would tell a friend.

1. Tap **AI Product Assistant** in the admin menu.
2. Describe the item in your own words, for example:

   > *"Add a red floral dress, KSh 3,500, sizes M to XL, 5 in stock. Put it under Dresses."*

3. The assistant prepares a **draft**: it writes the details, attaches an image,
   and picks one of your existing categories.
4. If something is missing (like the price), it will **ask you** for it.
5. **You review the draft** — check the name, price, sizes, stock and picture.
   Nothing goes to the shop until you approve it.
6. Tap **Publish** when you are happy.

The product is now in your shop. You can always edit it later under **Products**.

## 5. Upload clothing photos

- In the product form, tap **Upload product images**.
- Choose photos from your phone or computer (JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB each, max 10).
- The **first photo is the cover photo** shown in the shop.
- Tip: take photos in good light, show the full item, avoid cluttered backgrounds.

## 6. Update stock (when new stock arrives or it's sold)

1. Go to **Products** and tap the product.
2. Change the **Stock quantity** number.
3. Tap **Save**.

You never need to recreate the product — the photos, description, price and
everything else stay attached.

**Good to know:**

- When a customer buys, the stock **goes down by itself** (buy 2, stock 10 → 8).
- When stock reaches **0**, the product shows **OUT OF STOCK** and customers
  cannot buy it until you add more.
- You can set a **low-stock alert** level (e.g. alert me at 3 left).

## 7. Change prices

1. Go to **Products** and tap the product.
2. Change the **Price (KSh)**.
3. Tap **Save**.

The new price shows in the shop immediately.

## 8. Manage orders

When a customer places an order you will see it immediately under **Orders**.

Each order shows: customer name, phone, what they bought, how many, subtotal,
delivery fee, total, payment method, payment status, order status, and — for
M-Pesa orders — the **transaction code**.

**For M-Pesa orders (very important):**

1. The order arrives showing **"Awaiting verification"**.
2. Open **your own M-Pesa** (your phone) and check that you actually **received
   the money** — match the amount and the transaction code shown on the order.
3. Only when you have confirmed the money is in your account, open the order
   and tap **Verify payment — mark as Paid**.
   - The website **never receives the money directly** — money goes straight to
     your M-Pesa. You are simply confirming what you received.
4. If a code does not match anything you received, you can mark it
   **Failed** or **Cancelled** and contact the customer.

**Moving an order along:** open the order, choose the **New status**
(Pending → Confirmed → Processing → Ready → Shipped → Delivered) and tap
**Update status**. Every change is recorded in the order's history.

If a customer cancels, mark the order **Cancelled**.

## 9. Set up your M-Pesa Till / Paybill (one time)

This is where customers see **your** payment details when paying.

1. Go to **Settings** → find the **Payments** section.
2. Choose how you want to be paid:
   - **Pay on delivery** — customer pays cash when the order arrives.
   - **M-Pesa** — customer pays upfront to your Till or Paybill.
3. If you use M-Pesa, switch it on and enter:
   - **Business name** (the name customers see)
   - **M-Pesa Till** — switch on and type your Till number (if you have one)
   - **M-Pesa Paybill** — switch on and type your Paybill number and account
     name (if you use a Paybill instead)
   - **Payment instructions** — anything you want to tell customers
     (you can leave it empty; the shop shows simple standard steps automatically)
4. Tap **Save changes**.

Your customers will then see exactly your Till/Paybill at checkout, pay you
directly, and type their M-Pesa confirmation code on the order. You verify it
in your own M-Pesa (see section 8).

> There is **no payment company in the middle and no extra fees** — money goes
> straight from the customer to your M-Pesa.

## 10. Change your WhatsApp number

1. Go to **Settings** → **Contact** section.
2. Update **WhatsApp number** (write it with the country code, e.g. 2547…).
3. Tap **Save changes**.

Every WhatsApp button on the shop — chat buttons, order messages, payment
confirmations — immediately uses the new number. Nothing else to do.

## 11. Set your delivery zones and fees

Tell the shop which areas you deliver to and how much each costs.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Delivery** section.
2. Tap **Add zone**, type the area name (e.g. "Nairobi") and the fee
   (e.g. 250).
3. Add as many zones as you like.
4. Tap **Save changes**.

At checkout, customers pick their zone and the fee is added automatically.
If a customer's area is not in your list, the shop says the delivery fee will
be confirmed with them — you can then agree on the fee by phone or WhatsApp.

## 12. See your sales and analytics

- **Dashboard** (the first page after sign-in): today's numbers at a glance —
  orders, revenue, low stock alerts.
- **Analytics**: best sellers, top categories, sales over time.
- **Customers**: everyone who has ordered from you, with their contact details.

---

## Quick daily routine

1. **Morning:** sign in → check **Orders** for new orders → verify M-Pesa
   payments → confirm the orders.
2. **When packing:** update the status (Processing → Shipped as you go).
3. **When new stock arrives:** update the stock numbers under **Products**.

## If something looks wrong

- A customer says they paid but you never received the money? → Do **not** mark
  it Paid. Contact them on WhatsApp and ask for the correct transaction code.
- You forgot your password? → Contact your web support person to reset it.
- Anything else? → Contact your web support person.

Asante sana, and happy selling! 🌸
